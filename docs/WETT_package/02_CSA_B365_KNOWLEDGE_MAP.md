## Executive Summary

CSA B365:25, *Installation code for solid-fuel-burning appliances and equipment*, is the current ninth edition of the CSA standard. It was published in April 2025 and supersedes the 2017 edition, including the reaffirmed B365-17 (R2022).

The 2025 edition identifies significant revisions relating to:

- shielding;
- flue pipes;
- clearances;
- alcove shielding;
- reference publications; and
- a new informative annex on decommissioning.

WETT currently sells **CSA B365-25** and its 2026 training/conference material specifically addresses **B365 2025 updates and interpretations**, confirming that the 2025 edition is operationally relevant to current WETT training.

CSA B365:25 covers installation, alteration, and maintenance of solid-fuel-burning appliances and equipment installed indoors or outdoors. Its stated appliance scope includes furnaces, boilers, stoves, ranges, space heaters, factory-built fireplaces, and water heaters.

A crucial boundary is that CSA B365:25 **does not apply to site-built fireplaces**. Therefore:

- a masonry chimney serving an appliance can fall within the B365 venting map;
- a fireplace insert installed into an existing fireplace is specifically addressed by B365;
- but the construction requirements for a traditional site-built masonry fireplace itself must be sourced from the applicable building code and other governing documents, not invented from B365.

The inspection map is concentrated in:

- **Clause 4 — General requirements**
- **Clause 5 — Air for combustion and ventilation**
- **Clause 6 — Venting flue gases**
- **Clause 7 — Controls and safety devices**
- **Clause 8 — Installation of appliances**
- **Clause 9 — Installation of ducts**
- **Clause 10 — Tests**
- **Clause 11 — Add-on furnaces / boilers**
- **Annex E — Chimney inspection (informative)**

For WETT field use, the standard should be treated as one layer in a hierarchy:

**Appliance identification / certification  
→ manufacturer’s certified instructions  
→ applicable CSA B365 clause  
→ chimney/component listing and instructions  
→ provincial building/fire code adoption  
→ municipal process where applicable  
→ observed field condition**

# CSA B365:25 Scope Boundary

## Included appliance types

The current scope includes solid-fuel-burning:

- furnaces;
- boilers;
- stoves;
- ranges;
- space heaters;
- factory-built fireplaces;
- water heaters.

The solid-fuel definition includes several biomass/solid fuel types, including cordwood and pellets.

## Excluded by the stated scope

CSA B365:25 states that it does not apply to:

- incinerators;
- site-built fireplaces;
- process equipment;
- portable appliances.

### Inspection significance

This prevents a major WETT knowledge error:

**Do not use B365 as though it contains the construction code for a site-built masonry fireplace.**

For a masonry fireplace inspection:

- B365 may govern an insert, hearth-mounted appliance, flue pipe, or other appliance-related portion;
- chimney and fireplace construction requirements may come from building-code provisions;
- manufacturer instructions may govern an installed insert or factory-built system.

# Verified Knowledge — Appliance Installation Map

## 1. Appliance Listing / Certification / Suitability

**Jurisdiction:** Canada — CSA B365 common layer  
**Business:** BOTH  
**System:** Solid-fuel-burning appliance  
**Classification:** CODE / STANDARD REQUIREMENT MAP  
**Primary Clause Map:** 4.1, 4.2, 4.5, 4.9.1  
**Related:** Clause 2 references; manufacturer certification/listing standard

### Inspection meaning

The inspector must first determine what appliance is actually installed and whether it is suitable for the use/configuration observed.

Inspection questions:

- Is the manufacturer known?
- Is the model known?
- Is a certification/listing label present and legible?
- What installation configuration was the appliance tested/listed for?
- Is the appliance being used in a way consistent with its listing?
- Is the installation altered from the tested configuration?
- Is an uncertified appliance being evaluated?

### What to photograph

- Certification/data label.
- Manufacturer/model/serial information.
- Overall appliance and installation configuration.
- Any altered or unidentified component relevant to listing.

### Escalation

If the appliance cannot be positively identified:

**MANUFACTURER LOOKUP REQUIRED**

If the applicable listing/configuration cannot be established:

**NOT VERIFIED**

# 4. Clearance Reduction / Wall and Ceiling Shielding

**Primary Clause:** 8.2.4  
**Related:** 8.2.1–8.2.3  
**Edition note:** The 2025 preface specifically identifies revised requirements for shielding, clearances, and alcove shielding.

### Inspection significance

When reduced clearances are claimed, inspect:

- whether reduction is allowed for that appliance/configuration;
- type of shield;
- shield material;
- mounting method;
- air-space/ventilation configuration;
- coverage;
- top/bottom/edge ventilation;
- fasteners/spacers;
- whether manufacturer-specified shielding is present;
- whether the claimed reduced clearance is supported.

### Red flag

A sheet of tile, metal, cement board, or masonry placed directly on a combustible wall must not automatically be treated as a compliant clearance-reduction system.

The actual assembly must be validated against the governing requirement.

# Fireplace Knowledge Map

# 40. Site-Built / Masonry Fireplace

**CSA B365:25 Scope:** EXCLUDED as a site-built fireplace.

### What this means

Do **not** use B365 as the primary construction standard for:

- masonry fireplace firebox construction;
- smoke chamber construction;
- masonry fireplace damper construction;
- masonry hearth structural construction;
- fireplace masonry dimensions.

Those require the applicable building-code/fireplace provisions.

### But B365 can still become relevant where:

- an insert is installed in the fireplace;
- a hearth-mounted stove is installed;
- an appliance connects into the chimney;
- liner/flue-pipe requirements apply;
- a covered solid-fuel appliance uses the masonry chimney.

# Inspection Application

## Field Decision Sequence

### Step 1 — Identify the system

Record:

- appliance type;
- manufacturer;
- model;
- certification;
- fuel;
- fireplace/chimney type;
- flue-pipe type;
- liner type;
- age/installation date if known.

### Step 2 — Establish jurisdiction

- Ontario → later use WETT Core + Apollo Ontario.
- Alberta → later use WETT Core + Phoenix Alberta.

### Step 3 — Establish applicable standard edition

Do not assume B365:25 is the legally adopted edition.

### Step 4 — Find manufacturer instructions

Required whenever appliance/system listing is relevant.

### Step 5 — Use this knowledge map

Locate the B365 clause family for the observed issue.

### Step 6 — Inspect / measure / photograph

Only according to actual SITE inspection level/access.

### Step 7 — Verify exact requirement

Use:
- licensed applicable B365 edition;
- manufacturer instructions;
- province code;
- product standard/listing;
- municipal requirement if applicable.

### Step 8 — Classify report finding

Do not issue a compliance conclusion from this map alone.

# Reporting Guidance

## Correct source wording

Preferred:

> The observed condition relates to CSA B365 Clause 6.4 requirements for flue-pipe installation. Final compliance classification is based on the applicable adopted edition and the appliance/manufacturer requirements.

Avoid:

> “CSA says all stove pipe must always be ___.”

unless the exact current/applicable clause has been verified for the installed system.

---

## Manufacturer-dependent wording

> The observed configuration requires verification against the appliance manufacturer’s certified installation instructions. The model-specific requirement has not been established from the general CSA B365 map alone.

---

## Site-built fireplace wording

> CSA B365 excludes site-built fireplaces from its stated scope. The masonry fireplace construction item must be evaluated against the applicable building-code/fireplace requirements. Appliance, insert, liner, or chimney-related portions may be governed by separate B365 provisions.

---

## Edition-control wording

> CSA B365:25 is the current CSA edition; however, the edition legally applicable to this installation must be determined from the governing provincial code/regulation and any transition provisions.

---

# Exceptions / Edge Cases

## 1. New B365 edition but older provincial reference

Do not silently upgrade the legal requirement.

Record:
- current CSA edition;
- adopted provincial edition;
- installation date;
- alteration date;

Use the actual certified installation instructions where applicable.
